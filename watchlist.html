The Watchlist

Creator profiles behind the videos we share.

The Watchlist tracks the people and channels behind useful AI videos. Profiles collect source links, teaching focus, credibility signals, and Aesop Academy course paths connected to the topics they cover.

Video pulls should enrich creator profiles here. The videos themselves belong in the curated video library, not on the Watchlist.

Profiles, not video cards.

When a video is added to 25experts, the Watchlist should preserve what we can learn about the creator: who they are, where their work lives, what topics they teach well, and which Aesop Academy courses can continue the learning path.
